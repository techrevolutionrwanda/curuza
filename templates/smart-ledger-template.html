Usability rules

  • Green = money in & OK. Gold = highlight. Amber = attention. Red = money owed.
  • 52px minimum touch targets and one primary action per screen.
  • Never rely on colour alone: status badges always carry a word.
  • Kinyarwanda first, English as subtitle; amounts as “12,500 RWF”.
  • Sync chip always visible so owners trust offline mode.
  • Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on every surface.
